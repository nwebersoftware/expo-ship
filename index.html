Tools

app-check

One command that runs the right pre-flight on your build — Android .aab/.apk, iOS .ipa, or a PNG icon — and aggregates the verdict.

npx @njweber/app-check --dir ./dist

store-check

A dependency-free CLI that checks an Android .aab/.apk for the Google Play 16 KB page-size requirement — and exits non-zero in CI if a native lib is misaligned.

npx @njweber/store-check ./app-release.aab

manifest-check

Audits the Android manifest from an .aab or text AndroidManifest.xml — debuggable, cleartext, exported components, permissions.

npx @njweber/android-manifest-check ./app-release.aab

ipa-check

Audits an iOS .ipa for App Store submission pitfalls — build numbers, the export-compliance key, the privacy manifest, and iPad orientation gaps.

npx @njweber/ipa-check ./YourApp.ipa

privacy-check

Scans an iOS binary for privacy-sensitive APIs and reports which required Info.plist usage-description keys are missing.

npx @njweber/privacy-check ./YourApp.ipa

icon-check

Validates a PNG app icon — 1024×1024, square, and no alpha channel (the classic App Store rejection), or adaptive-icon foreground rules for Android.

npx @njweber/icon-check ./assets/icon.png

screenshot-check

Validates a folder of store screenshots — size, format, aspect ratio and count — for Google Play or the App Store.

npx @njweber/screenshot-check ./screenshots
